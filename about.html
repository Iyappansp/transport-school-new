Milestones & Track Record

Our Fleet Evolution & Zero-Incident Journey.

From a 5-bus pilot in 2021 to managing 45+ connected school coaches serving over 10,000 students daily across 18 premier partner schools.

The SafeRoute Safety

Launched a 5-bus pilot introducing mandatory background verification, female crossing attendants on every bus, and instant SMS boarding alerts.

Equipped 100% of our fleet with 4G GPS trackers, electronic RFID card readers, automated speed governors locked at 40 km/h, and parent mobile portal.
