Help & FAQs

Questions Parents Ask Before Choosing School Transport.

Find answers about routes, safety, tracking, subscriptions and daily transport.

General

Parents select a school and pickup area, review matching routes, and submit a request. Once assigned, the route and pickup stop appear in the parent portal.
Yes. Your child's route, stop and timings are listed under "My Child" in the parent portal.
Stops are assigned based on the pickup area you provide and the routes available for your child's school.

Routes

You can submit a preferred pickup area, and our transport team will confirm the closest available stop on a matching route.
Route changes can be requested from the Subscription page in the parent portal and are subject to availability.
If a route shows limited availability, our transport team will suggest the nearest alternative route and stop.

Safety

Drivers go through an onboarding and documentation process before being assigned to a route. See the Safety page for details.
Where an attendant is assigned to a route, they support students during boarding, travel and drop-off.
Routine inspection and maintenance checks are part of everyday vehicle readiness before a route begins.

Tracking

Yes. The parent portal shows the assigned bus's current position, next stop and estimated arrival time.
Parents receive an update when the bus is approaching the pickup stop and again on arrival at school.
Boarding confirmation appears in the parent portal shortly after your child boards the bus.
The ETA shown in the parent portal updates to reflect the bus's current progress along the route.

Subscriptions

Monthly plans are billed each month based on the plan you select at sign-up.
Yes, plans can be changed from the Subscription page in the parent portal.
A pause request can be submitted from the Subscription page and is reviewed by the transport team.

Payments

Yes, the Payments page in the parent portal lists previous transport payments and their status.
Every payment generates a digital receipt that can be viewed or downloaded from the Receipts page.

Support

Reach us through the Contact page, by phone during support hours, or by email at support@example.com.
Transport issues can be reported through the Contact page and will be reviewed by our transport support team.
