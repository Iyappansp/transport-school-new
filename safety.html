A Safer Journey Starts Before the Bus Moves.

Responsible people, clear procedures, route visibility and communication work together to support dependable student transportation.

Driver onboarding and verification procedures help transport operators establish appropriate documentation and role readiness before assigning drivers to routes.
Where an attendant is assigned, they support students during boarding, travel and drop-off.
Everyday vehicle readiness matters — routine inspection, maintenance checks, vehicle cleanliness, safety equipment review and route readiness are part of daily preparation.
Route position is shared with the parent portal so families can follow the journey from pickup to drop-off.
Boarding and alighting are logged daily, giving parents a confirmed record of their child's journey.
Journeys are observed from pickup through school arrival and again on the return route, supporting a consistent daily schedule.
